# Duck Dash Escape

🦆 DUCK ESCAPE

The farm wants you back. RUN.

Duck Escape is a simple, funny retro-style arcade game where you play as a duck that has escaped from a farm. Your goal is to get as far away from the farm as possible without getting caught or hit by obstacles.

🎮 How the game works

The player controls a small duck using WASD or the arrow keys.

The duck starts on the farm and has to move forward through different areas. The screen can continuously scroll, giving the feeling that the duck is escaping farther and farther away.

Along the way:

 🍞 Collect bread → +10 points

 🚜 Avoid tractors → getting hit costs one life

 🐕 Avoid farm dogs → they can chase the duck for a short time

 🧑‍🌾 Avoid the farmer → if he catches you, you lose a life

 🪨 Avoid obstacles → fences, rocks, hay bales, puddles etc.

 ❤️ Start with 3 lives

 ⭐ Your score increases the longer you survive

 ⚡ The game gradually becomes faster and harder

🗺️ Levels

LEVEL 1 — THE FARM
Escape through fields while avoiding tractors, fences and the farmer.

LEVEL 2 — THE ROAD
Why did the duck cross the road?

Cross roads while avoiding cars and trucks. Bread can appear between lanes for extra points.

LEVEL 3 — THE FOREST
Run through the forest while avoiding trees, rocks and animals.

LEVEL 4 — FREEDOM?
The duck thinks it has finally escaped...

Then something ridiculous can happen, like:

🚨 FARMER BOSS INCOMING

The farmer appears on a giant tractor and chases the duck. 😂

💀 Game Over

When all three lives are gone:

GAME OVER

The farm got you.

I'd make it look like an old 80s/90s arcade game rather than a modern polished game: pixel-art duck, chunky text, simple animations, retro sound effects, hearts for lives and a high-score counter.
